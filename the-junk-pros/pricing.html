How Our Pricing Works

Volume-based rates that are easy to understand

📏

Volume-Based

You only pay for the space your items take up in our truck. We measure in fractions: 1/8 load, 1/4 load, 1/2 load, 3/4 load, or full truck load.

💯

All-Inclusive

Our quote includes everything: labor, loading, hauling, disposal fees, recycling costs, and cleanup. No add-ons or surprise charges.

👀

Upfront Quote

We provide your quote on-site before we start work. See exactly what you'll pay, then decide if you want to proceed. Zero obligation.

Understanding truck load sizes

1/8 Truck Load

Examples:

1/4 Truck Load

1/2 Truck Load

Full Truck Load

Examples:

  • Complete house cleanout
  • Full estate cleanout
  • Large garage/storage unit
  • Major renovation debris

Typical items: Entire home or large project

Factors That Affect Your Price

What we consider when providing your quote

Volume of Items

This is the primary factor. More items = more truck space = higher price. Less junk = lower price. Simple and fair.

Type of Materials

Some items cost more to dispose of properly (electronics, appliances with refrigerant, tires). We'll let you know if any items have special disposal fees.

Location Accessibility

Items on the first floor or in a garage are standard pricing. Upper floors or difficult access may require additional labor.

Service Area Distance

Standard pricing applies to our primary service area around Kerrville. Extended areas may have a small travel fee, which we'll mention when you call.

Our No Hidden Fees Guarantee

The price we quote on-site is the final price you pay. No fuel surcharges, no disposal fees added later, no "gotcha" moments. If we quote $200, you pay $200. Period.
