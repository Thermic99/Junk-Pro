Frequently Asked Questions - Junction Junk Removal

How far in advance should I schedule Junction pickups?

We recommend scheduling Junction pickups 2-3 days in advance when possible. This allows us to coordinate trips efficiently. However, we can sometimes accommodate same-day or next-day service depending on our schedule.

Is there an extra charge for Junction service?

Due to the distance, there may be a travel fee for Junction pickups. We'll always provide an upfront quote so you know the total cost before we begin. For larger jobs, the travel fee becomes a smaller percentage of the overall cost.

Can you service hunting properties and remote ranches?

Absolutely! We're experienced with Hill Country ranch properties and can access remote locations. Just let us know about access conditions when you call, and we'll come prepared with the right equipment.

Do you serve the entire Kimble County area?

Yes! We serve Junction and the surrounding Kimble County area including London, Segovia, and Telegraph. If you're unsure about your location, give us a call and we'll let you know if we can help.
