Serving Mountain Home and Surrounding Ranch Country

Mountain Home sits in some of the most beautiful ranch country in Texas, where large properties are the norm and neighbors might be miles apart. We understand what that means for junk removal - properties here often have multiple outbuildings, barns, and storage areas that accumulate items over decades. Getting rid of it all can feel overwhelming, but that's where we come in.

Located about 20 miles north of Kerrville along Highway 41, Mountain Home is well within our regular service area. We know the roads, we understand the properties, and we have the equipment to handle whatever your Hill Country ranch or home has accumulated.

Why Mountain Home Residents Trust The Junk Pros

Living in Mountain Home means embracing the rural Hill Country lifestyle - wide open spaces, wildlife, and a slower pace of life. But it also means properties tend to hold onto things longer. That old tractor behind the barn. The furniture from when you remodeled the lodge. The appliances you replaced but never hauled off. We've seen it all, and we can help you clear it all.

Our team respects your property and your time. We show up when we say we will, provide honest upfront pricing, and leave your space cleaner than we found it. Whether you need a single item removed or a complete property cleanout, we approach every job with the same professionalism.

Ranch Property Specialists

Mountain Home properties often have unique junk removal needs. If it's taking up space on your property and you want it gone, give us a call. We'll work with you to find the right solution.
