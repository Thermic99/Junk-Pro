Garage Cleanout Pricing

Affordable, volume-based pricing

Our garage cleanout pricing is based on how much space your items take up in our truck. This means you only pay for what you need removed - no flat fees for services you don't use.

Typical Pricing Ranges:

  • Lightly cluttered garage: $150-250
  • Moderately full garage: $250-400
  • Packed single-car garage: $300-450
  • Packed two-car garage: $450-700
  • Hoarding situation: Custom pricing based on assessment

What Affects Garage Cleanout Cost?

  • Volume of items: More stuff = higher cost
  • Type of items: Heavy materials like concrete or machinery cost more than boxes and light debris
  • Hazardous materials: Paint, chemicals, and batteries may require special disposal (small additional fee)
  • Wall-mounted fixtures: Removing shelving, cabinets, or pegboards adds time
  • Donation sorting: Extra time for sorting donations versus disposal

Most garage cleanouts fall in the $250-400 range. Call (830) 285-4281 for an accurate quote!

Garage Cleanout FAQ

Common questions answered

How much does a garage cleanout cost in Kerrville? +
Garage cleanout costs typically range from $200-600 depending on the amount of clutter. A lightly filled single-car garage might be $200-300, while a packed two-car garage could be $400-600. We provide free quotes after seeing what needs removal.
How long does a garage cleanout take? +
Most garage cleanouts take 1-3 hours depending on how full the garage is and what types of items need removal. We work efficiently to minimize disruption to your day.
Do I need to sort through items before you arrive? +
No! You can simply point to what you want gone and what you want to keep. If you prefer to have everything removed and sorted later, we can do that too. We work with whatever process is easiest for you.
Can you remove items attached to the garage walls? +
Yes, we can remove wall-mounted shelving, pegboards, cabinets, and other garage fixtures. Just let us know what you want removed when we provide your quote.
Do you offer same-day garage cleanout service? +
Yes! Call us before noon and we can typically schedule same-day garage cleanout service throughout Kerrville and the Hill Country, subject to availability.
What do you do with items you remove from garages? +
We donate usable items to local charities, recycle materials like metal and cardboard, and properly dispose of the rest. We try to keep as much as possible out of landfills.
Can you remove hazardous materials like paint and chemicals? +
We can remove household quantities of paint, oil, and common garage chemicals for proper disposal at certified facilities. Large quantities or highly hazardous materials may require specialized services.
