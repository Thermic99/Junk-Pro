Professional Hot Tub Removal Service

That old hot tub taking up valuable space in your backyard? Whether it's broken, outdated, or you're simply ready for a change, The Junk Pros specializes in complete hot tub and spa removal throughout Kerrville and the Texas Hill Country.

Hot tub removal is a big job. These units can weigh 500-1,000 pounds when empty and are often located in challenging spots - behind narrow gates, on elevated decks, or in tight corners of your yard. Many hot tubs are hardwired to electrical systems and require careful disconnection. This isn't a DIY project for most homeowners.

Our experienced team has removed hundreds of hot tubs across the Hill Country. We have the equipment, expertise, and muscle to handle hot tubs of any size and in any location. Whether your spa is a standard 6-person model on a concrete pad or a massive swim spa on a second-story deck, we can remove it safely and efficiently.

We serve all of Kerrville, Ingram, Comfort, Fredericksburg, Boerne, and surrounding Hill Country communities. As a locally owned business, we understand the unique properties and landscapes in our area - from ranch properties to residential neighborhoods to Hill Country estates.

What's Included in Our Hot Tub Removal Service

Complete service from start to finish

Disconnection Assistance

We can disconnect plug-in hot tubs ourselves. For hardwired units, we'll coordinate with you on electrical disconnection (which must be done by a licensed electrician). We guide you through the entire process.

Demolition & Dismantling

Most hot tubs need to be cut apart to fit through gates and tight spaces. We handle the entire demolition process using proper tools and safety equipment. Your property stays protected throughout.

Complete Removal

We haul away every piece of your old hot tub - the shell, cabinet panels, equipment, plumbing, and debris. Nothing gets left behind. We also remove the cover, steps, and any accessories you want gone.

Site Cleanup

After removal, we sweep and clean the area where your hot tub was located. We haul away all debris and leave your yard neat and ready for whatever comes next.

Our Hot Tub Removal Process

Step-by-step professional service

Initial Assessment & Quote

Call us at (830) 285-4281 to schedule an on-site assessment. We'll evaluate your hot tub's size, location, and accessibility. Based on this assessment, we provide a free, upfront quote with no hidden fees. Most hot tub removals range from $300-600.

Preparation

Before we arrive, you'll need to drain your hot tub completely and arrange for electrical disconnection if it's hardwired. We'll provide detailed instructions and can recommend local electricians if needed. Simple plug-in units can be disconnected by us.

Demolition & Removal

On removal day, our team arrives with the necessary equipment and tools. We carefully dismantle and cut your hot tub as needed to navigate it through gates and around obstacles. We load everything onto our truck, sweep up all debris, and restore your yard.

Responsible Disposal

We transport your old hot tub to proper disposal facilities. Acrylic shells, cabinet materials, and metal components are separated and recycled when possible. We follow all Texas environmental regulations for disposal.

Hot Tub Removal Pricing

Transparent pricing for every situation

Hot tub removal pricing varies based on several factors. We provide free on-site quotes so you know exactly what to expect - no surprises or hidden fees.

What Affects Hot Tub Removal Cost?

Size & Type

Smaller 2-4 person hot tubs cost less to remove than large 8-person spas or swim spas. Standard models are less complex than luxury units with extensive features.

Location & Access

Hot tubs on ground level with wide gate access are easier to remove than units on elevated decks or behind narrow gates requiring extensive dismantling.

Condition

Hot tubs that can be moved intact (rare) cost less than units requiring complete demolition and cutting into pieces for removal.

Additional Items

Removal of gazebos, privacy screens, or decking around the hot tub will increase cost. We can handle these additions - just let us know.

Typical Pricing Ranges:

  • Small hot tubs (2-4 person): $300-400
  • Standard hot tubs (5-6 person): $350-450
  • Large hot tubs (7-8 person): $400-500
  • Swim spas & specialty: $500-800+

Hot Tub Removal FAQ

Your questions answered

How much does hot tub removal cost in Kerrville? +
Hot tub removal typically costs $300-600 depending on size, location, and complexity. Standard 6-8 person hot tubs average $350-450. Larger swim spas or difficult access locations may cost more. We provide free quotes after assessing your specific situation.
Do you handle hot tub disconnection? +
We can disconnect simple plug-in hot tubs. However, hardwired electrical connections must be disconnected by a licensed electrician before we arrive. We'll coordinate with you on the disconnection process.
Can you remove hot tubs from tight spaces? +
Yes! We have extensive experience removing hot tubs from backyards with narrow gates, elevated decks, and challenging access. In most cases, we can cut and dismantle the hot tub on-site to fit through tight spaces.
How long does hot tub removal take? +
Most hot tub removal jobs take 2-4 hours from start to finish. This includes draining (if needed), disconnection, demolition/dismantling, loading, and cleanup. Complex jobs may take longer.
Do I need to drain my hot tub before removal? +
Yes, hot tubs must be completely drained before we can remove them. Most homeowners can drain their hot tub using the built-in drain valve. We can provide guidance if you're unsure how to drain yours.
What do you do with the old hot tub after removal? +
We transport hot tubs to proper disposal facilities where materials are separated for recycling when possible. Metal components, electrical equipment, and some plastics can be recycled according to Texas environmental regulations.
Can you remove the concrete pad or deck under the hot tub? +
We focus on hot tub removal specifically. For concrete pad or deck removal, we can refer you to qualified contractors in the Hill Country area who specialize in that type of work.
