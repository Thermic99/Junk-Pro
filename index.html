Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our junk removal service

How much does junk removal cost? +

Our pricing is based on how much space your items take in our truck. Small loads start at $99, half-truck loads from $249, and full truck loads from $399. We provide free, no-obligation quotes before any work begins - the price we quote is the price you pay, with no hidden fees.

Do you offer same-day service? +

Yes! We offer same-day junk removal service throughout Kerrville and the Hill Country. Call us before noon and we can usually be there the same day. We're available 7 days a week from 7 AM to 7 PM.

What items do you take? +

We remove almost anything! Furniture, appliances, electronics, yard waste, construction debris, hot tubs, mattresses, and more. We even handle estate cleanouts and commercial junk removal. The only items we can't take are hazardous materials like paint, chemicals, and certain batteries.

Do I need to be home during the removal? +

You don't need to be home as long as the items are accessible and payment is arranged. However, many customers prefer to be present for the initial quote and to ensure we remove exactly what they want gone. We're flexible and work with your schedule.
