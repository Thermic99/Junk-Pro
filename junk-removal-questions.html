Junk Removal Questions Answered

Clear answers to common questions before you request a junk removal quote.

How much does junk removal cost?

A small load is $165, a half load is $350, and a full load starts at $700. The space your items take in the truck helps determine the load size. Discuss your items and quote before scheduling. See the load size and pricing guide.

How do I estimate my load size?

Make a list of the items you want removed and note their approximate sizes. Photos can help explain how much you have. Items vary in shape, so ask the team which load size fits your job.

What items can be removed?

The website lists furniture removal, appliance removal and estate cleanouts. Ask about your specific items before scheduling, particularly anything that might need special handling.

How should I prepare for a quote?

List the items and their locations. Mention stairs, access restrictions or large items, and keep belongings you want to retain separate from items to remove. Contact Steady Flame to discuss availability and the details of your job.

How can I plan an estate cleanout?

Start by setting aside important documents and keepsakes, then separate belongings by destination. Our estate cleanout checklist walks through the steps.
