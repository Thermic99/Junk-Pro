Furniture Removal Pricing

Transparent, volume-based pricing

At Steady Flame Hauling and Junk Removal, we believe in honest, upfront pricing. Our furniture removal rates are based on the volume of space your items take up in our truck - not by weight or individual item counts. This means you get fair pricing regardless of whether your couch is heavy or light.

What Affects Furniture Removal Cost?

Volume of Furniture

The more space your furniture occupies in our truck, the higher the cost. A single recliner will cost less than an entire living room set.

Accessibility

Furniture on the ground floor with easy access costs less than items requiring stair navigation or difficult removal from tight spaces.

Item Condition

Furniture in good condition that we can donate may qualify for a discount, as we can offset disposal costs through charitable partnerships.

Location

Service within Kerrville and immediate surrounding areas has standard pricing. Extended Hill Country locations may have a small travel fee.

Typical Pricing Ranges:

  • Single item (chair, nightstand): $50-100
  • Medium item (couch, mattress): $75-150
  • Large item (sectional, dining set): $150-250
  • Full room of furniture: $200-400
  • Multi-room or whole house: $400-800+

Prices vary based on specific items and circumstances. Call for an accurate quote!

Furniture Removal FAQ

Common questions about our furniture hauling service

How much does furniture removal cost in Kerrville? +
Furniture removal pricing is based on volume - you only pay for the space your furniture takes up in our truck. A single couch typically ranges from $75-150, while a full room of furniture might be $200-400. We provide free, upfront quotes with no hidden fees.
Do you remove mattresses and box springs? +
Yes! We remove all types of mattresses and box springs, including king, queen, full, and twin sizes. We handle proper disposal and recycling in accordance with Texas regulations.
Can you remove furniture from upstairs or a basement? +
Absolutely. Our team is experienced in removing furniture from all levels of your home, including upstairs bedrooms, basements, and attics. We handle all the heavy lifting and navigate stairs carefully to avoid damage to your property.
Do you donate furniture that's still in good condition? +
Yes! We partner with local Hill Country charities and donation centers. Any furniture in good, usable condition is donated rather than sent to the landfill. We'll let you know if your items qualify for donation.
How quickly can you remove my furniture? +
We offer same-day furniture removal service when you call before noon. Most furniture removal jobs take 30-60 minutes once we arrive. We provide convenient 2-hour arrival windows to accommodate your schedule.
Do I need to disassemble furniture before you arrive? +
No! We handle all necessary disassembly as part of our service. If a piece of furniture needs to be taken apart to fit through doorways or be loaded safely, we'll take care of it.
What areas do you serve for furniture removal? +
We serve all of Kerrville and the Texas Hill Country including Ingram, Hunt, Comfort, Fredericksburg, Boerne, Bandera, and surrounding communities. Call us to confirm service in your specific area.
