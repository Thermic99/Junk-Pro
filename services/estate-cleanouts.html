Compassionate Estate Cleanout Service

Losing a loved one is never easy. In the midst of grief and the complexities of settling an estate, the task of clearing out a lifetime of belongings can feel overwhelming. The Junk Pros provides compassionate, professional estate cleanout services throughout Kerrville and the Texas Hill Country to ease this burden during a difficult time.

We understand that estate cleanouts are deeply personal and emotionally challenging. This isn't just about removing items - it's about handling a loved one's possessions with care and respect. Our team approaches every estate cleanout with sensitivity, working at a pace that's comfortable for you and your family.

Whether you're a family member handling a loved one's estate, an executor managing multiple properties, an attorney coordinating estate settlement, or a real estate agent preparing a property for sale, we provide the full-service support you need. From sorting and organizing to donation coordination and final clearing, we handle every aspect of the estate cleanout process.

We serve families and professionals throughout the Hill Country including Kerrville, Ingram, Comfort, Fredericksburg, Boerne, Bandera, and all surrounding communities. As a locally owned business, we're your neighbors who understand the unique needs of Hill Country families during these difficult transitions.

Our Estate Cleanout Process

Organized, respectful, and thorough

Initial Consultation

We start with a phone conversation to understand your situation, timeline, and needs. We answer your questions and explain our process. This initial call helps us understand the scope and sensitivity of your particular situation.

On-Site Assessment and Quote

We visit the property to assess the estate and provide a detailed quote. We walk through every room, discuss what needs to be kept versus removed, and answer any questions. Our quotes are comprehensive with no hidden fees.

Sorting and Clearing

On cleanout day, our team arrives and begins the careful process of sorting and removing items. We work room by room, setting aside items you want to keep, preparing donations, and loading removal items. We work at a pace you're comfortable with.

Donation and Disposal

Usable items are donated to local Hill Country charities - we provide donation receipts for estate tax purposes. Remaining items are disposed of responsibly through proper channels. We recycle materials whenever possible.

Final Walkthrough

We do a final walkthrough with you to ensure everything meets your expectations. The property is left broom-clean and ready for whatever comes next - sale, lease, or family use.

Estate Cleanout Pricing

Transparent pricing for every situation

Estate cleanout costs vary significantly based on home size, contents, and specific needs. We provide free on-site assessments and detailed quotes before beginning any work.

Typical Pricing Ranges:

  • 1 bedroom apartment/condo: $500-1,000
  • 2-3 bedroom home: $1,000-2,500
  • 3-4 bedroom home: $2,000-4,000
  • Large estate (4+ bedrooms): $3,500-6,000+
  • Hoarding situations: Custom pricing based on assessment

What Affects Estate Cleanout Cost?

  • Home size: Square footage and number of rooms
  • Contents volume: Amount of belongings accumulated
  • Property condition: Level of organization and cleanliness
  • Accessibility: Multi-story homes, narrow stairs, etc.
  • Timeline: Rush jobs may incur premium fees
  • Special items: Pianos, safes, or other difficult items
  • Donation coordination: Time spent sorting for donations

Estate Cleanout FAQ

Common questions answered

How much does an estate cleanout cost in Kerrville? +
Estate cleanout costs vary based on home size and contents. A typical 2-3 bedroom home ranges from $800-2,000, while larger estates may be $2,000-5,000+. We provide free on-site quotes and work with your budget and timeline.
How long does an estate cleanout take? +
Most estate cleanouts take 1-3 days depending on home size and contents. Smaller homes can often be completed in one day, while larger estates or homes with significant accumulation may require multiple days. We work efficiently while remaining respectful.
Do you donate items from estate cleanouts? +
Yes. We partner with local Hill Country charities to donate usable furniture, household items, clothing, and other goods. We can provide donation receipts for tax purposes. Items of value can be set aside for family or estate sale.
Can you help sort through items during the cleanout? +
Absolutely. We understand that estate cleanouts often involve sorting keepsakes from disposable items. We work with families to identify items to keep, donate, or dispose of. We handle everything with care and respect.
Do you work with attorneys and real estate agents? +
Yes, we frequently work with estate attorneys, executors, and real estate agents throughout the Hill Country. We understand the legal and time-sensitive nature of estate cleanouts and can provide documentation as needed.
What if we find valuables during the cleanout? +
We immediately set aside any items of obvious value - jewelry, collectibles, important documents, etc. These are presented to you or the executor for review. We never assume anything is disposable without your approval.
Can you handle hoarding situations? +
Yes. We have experience with hoarding situations and approach them with extra sensitivity and care. These cleanouts require more time and specialized handling, but we're equipped to manage them compassionately and professionally.
