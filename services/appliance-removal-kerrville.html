Eco-Friendly Recycling and Disposal

Appliances contain valuable recyclable materials — steel, copper, aluminum, and other metals that should not end up in a landfill. They also contain components that require special handling, including refrigerants in cooling appliances that must be recovered under EPA regulations. Steady Flame Hauling and Junk Removal is committed to responsible appliance disposal in Kerrville. We work with certified recycling facilities to ensure metals are recovered, refrigerants are properly handled, and as little material as possible goes to waste. Working appliances in decent condition are donated to Hill Country charities that can put them to use.

Appliance Removal FAQ — Kerrville, TX

Questions from Kerrville homeowners

How much does appliance removal cost in Kerrville? +
A single appliance removal in Kerrville typically costs $75-150 depending on the item and accessibility. Multiple appliances removed at the same time receive volume pricing, which lowers the per-item cost. We provide free, upfront quotes with no hidden fees. As our home base, Kerrville has no travel surcharges.
Do you recycle old appliances in Kerrville? +
Yes! We prioritize eco-friendly disposal for all appliances. Metals like steel, copper, and aluminum are recycled. Refrigerants from cooling appliances are recovered in accordance with EPA regulations. Working appliances in good condition may be donated to local charities. We divert as much material from landfills as possible.
Can you disconnect appliances before removing them? +
We can disconnect most standard appliances including washers, dryers, refrigerators, and dishwashers. For appliances that require gas line disconnection or specialized electrical work, we recommend having a licensed plumber or electrician handle the disconnection before our arrival for safety reasons.
Do you remove old water heaters in Kerrville? +
Absolutely. Water heater removal is one of our most common appliance jobs in Kerrville, especially in older homes that are upgrading to newer, more efficient units. Once the water heater has been disconnected by a plumber, we handle the removal and proper disposal, including navigating tight utility closets and garages.
