Professional Garage Cleanouts in Canyon Lake, Texas

Canyon Lake is one of the most popular destinations in the Texas Hill Country, and many of the homes here — whether full-time residences or vacation properties — share a common problem: garages that have become overflow storage instead of functional space. Between lake gear, seasonal decorations, old furniture, and years of accumulated belongings, it is easy for a two-car garage to become completely unusable. Steady Flame Hauling and Junk Removal specializes in garage cleanouts throughout Canyon Lake and Comal County, turning cluttered garages back into the organized, usable spaces they were meant to be.

Lake House Garage Clutter Is Different

Garages at Canyon Lake properties tend to accumulate a unique mix of items. Old life jackets, deflated inflatables, broken coolers, tangled fishing gear, worn-out dock accessories, and outgrown water toys pile up season after season. Add in the usual household overflow — boxes of old belongings, broken furniture, outdated appliances, and forgotten projects — and the garage quickly becomes impassable. Our team has seen it all in Canyon Lake garages, and we know how to sort through it efficiently, identifying anything worth keeping or donating and hauling everything else away.

Vacation Property and Seasonal Homeowner Services

Many Canyon Lake homeowners split their time between their lake property and a primary residence elsewhere. That makes it easy for the garage to become a dumping ground for items that do not quite fit inside the house. If you are preparing your Canyon Lake property for a new season, getting it ready to sell or rent, or simply tired of the clutter that greets you every time you visit, we can help. We coordinate easily with property managers and can work while you are away, sending photos before and after so you see the transformation.

Reclaim Your Space at Canyon Lake

Your Canyon Lake garage should hold your vehicles, your boat trailer, or at least give you room to work on projects — not serve as a storage unit you dread opening. Steady Flame Hauling and Junk Removal makes garage cleanouts simple: we arrive on time, give you an upfront price, and clear the entire space in a matter of hours. We sweep up afterward so you are left with a clean, empty garage ready for whatever you need it for next.

Garage Cleanout FAQ — Canyon Lake, TX

Questions from Canyon Lake homeowners

How much does a garage cleanout cost in Canyon Lake? +
Garage cleanout pricing in Canyon Lake is based on the volume of items we remove. A partial garage cleanout typically runs $150-300, while a full two-car garage packed floor to ceiling might range from $350-600. We provide a free, upfront quote on-site before any work begins. Canyon Lake is within our standard service area with no extra travel fees.
Can you clean out a vacation property garage at Canyon Lake? +
Absolutely. We frequently work with Canyon Lake vacation property owners who need garages cleared between seasons or before selling. We can coordinate with property managers or meet you at the property. Our team handles everything from start to finish, so you don't need to be on-site for the entire process.
Do you remove old boat gear and water sports equipment? +
Yes! Living near Canyon Lake means garages often accumulate old life jackets, broken coolers, worn tubes and inflatables, outdated fishing gear, and other water sports equipment. We haul it all away. If items are still in usable condition, we donate them to local organizations.
How quickly can you clean out a garage in Canyon Lake? +
Most Canyon Lake garage cleanouts are completed in 1-3 hours depending on the volume of items. We offer same-day service when you call before noon. Our crew arrives with a two-hour window, assesses the garage, provides your quote, and gets right to work once you approve.
