Commercial Junk Removal FAQ

Common questions from Hill Country businesses

Do you offer after-hours commercial junk removal? +
Yes! We understand businesses have specific scheduling needs. We can arrange after-hours, weekend, or early morning pickups to minimize disruption to your operations. Just let us know your preferred timing when scheduling.
How much does commercial junk removal cost in Kerrville? +
Commercial pricing is based on volume - you pay for the space your items take in our truck. We offer competitive rates for businesses and can provide volume discounts for large cleanouts. Call for a free, no-obligation quote.
Can you remove office furniture and electronics? +
Absolutely! We remove all types of office furniture including desks, chairs, cubicles, conference tables, and filing cabinets. We also handle electronics like computers, monitors, and printers, ensuring proper recycling when possible.
Do you offer recurring commercial junk removal services? +
Yes! We work with many Hill Country businesses on regular schedules - weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly pickups. This is popular with contractors, property managers, and retail businesses. Contact us to set up a recurring service plan.
Are you insured for commercial work? +
Yes, The Junk Pros is fully licensed and insured for both residential and commercial junk removal. We carry liability insurance to protect your business property during our service.
Can you provide documentation for tax or accounting purposes? +
Yes! We provide detailed invoices and can furnish donation receipts for items we donate on your behalf. This documentation can be useful for tax deductions and business record-keeping.
What areas do you serve for commercial junk removal? +
We serve businesses throughout the Texas Hill Country including Kerrville, Fredericksburg, Boerne, Bandera, Comfort, and surrounding areas. We regularly work with businesses along the I-10 corridor and can accommodate locations throughout the region.
