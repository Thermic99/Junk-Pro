Our Appliance Removal Process

Safe, efficient, and environmentally responsible

Disconnect Your Appliances

Before we arrive, please ensure all appliances are disconnected from gas, water, and electrical connections. For safety and liability reasons, we cannot disconnect utilities - you'll need a licensed plumber or electrician if professional disconnection is required. Many homeowners can handle simple disconnections themselves.

We Arrive and Quote

Call us at (830) 285-4281 to schedule your appointment. We arrive during your chosen 2-hour window, assess the appliances you need removed, and provide an upfront quote. Our pricing is based on the number and size of appliances - no hidden fees or surprise charges.

Safe Removal and Recycling

Once you approve the quote, our team carefully removes your appliances, handling all the heavy lifting and maneuvering. We transport them to certified recycling facilities where refrigerants are properly recovered, metals are recycled, and hazardous materials are disposed of according to Texas and EPA regulations.

Eco-Friendly Appliance Disposal

Protecting our Hill Country environment

Appliances contain materials that can harm the environment if improperly disposed of. At Steady Flame Hauling and Junk Removal, we take environmental responsibility seriously:

Refrigerant Recovery

Refrigerators, freezers, and AC units contain refrigerants that contribute to ozone depletion if released. We work with EPA-certified technicians who properly recover and recycle these substances.

Metal Recycling

The metal components in appliances - steel, copper, aluminum - are valuable recyclable materials. We ensure these metals are processed at certified recycling facilities, reducing landfill waste and conserving natural resources.

Hazardous Material Handling

Some appliances contain mercury switches, PCBs, or other hazardous materials. We follow all Texas and federal regulations for the safe handling and disposal of these substances.

Donation When Possible

Working appliances in good condition are donated to local Hill Country families through our partnerships with charitable organizations, giving them a second life instead of ending up in a landfill.

Appliance Removal FAQ

Common questions answered

How much does appliance removal cost in Kerrville? +
Appliance removal typically costs $50-150 per appliance depending on size and type. Smaller items like microwaves may be $50-75, while large refrigerators or commercial appliances may be $100-150. We provide free quotes with no obligation.
Do you recycle old appliances? +
Yes! We recycle appliances in accordance with Texas and federal regulations. Refrigerators and AC units have their refrigerant properly recovered by certified technicians. Metal components are recycled at local scrap facilities.
Can you remove built-in appliances? +
We can remove most built-in appliances that are already disconnected from utilities. If your appliance requires disconnection from gas, water, or electrical systems, you'll need a licensed plumber or electrician to handle that before we arrive.
Do I need to disconnect my appliances before you arrive? +
Yes, for safety and liability reasons, all appliances must be disconnected from gas, water, and electrical connections before removal. We can move and haul disconnected appliances, but we cannot perform utility disconnections.
What appliances do you remove? +
We remove all types of appliances including refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, dishwashers, stoves, ovens, water heaters, AC units, microwaves, and more. If it's an appliance, we can haul it away.
Do you offer same-day appliance removal? +
Yes! Call us before noon and we can typically schedule same-day appliance removal service throughout Kerrville and the Hill Country area, subject to availability.
Can you remove appliances from upstairs? +
Yes, our team is experienced in safely removing appliances from all levels of homes and buildings, including upstairs locations and tight spaces. We use proper equipment and techniques to protect your property during removal.
