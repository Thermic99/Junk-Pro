Furniture Removal FAQ — Boerne, TX

Common questions from Boerne homeowners

How much does furniture removal cost in Boerne, TX? +
Furniture removal in Boerne is based on the volume your items occupy in our truck. A single couch typically costs $75-150, while clearing a full room of furniture runs $200-400. Boerne is within our regular service area, so there are no extra travel charges. We provide free, upfront quotes with no hidden fees.
Can you pick up furniture from Main Street businesses in Boerne? +
Absolutely! We regularly help Main Street shops, offices, and restaurants remove old furniture. We understand the tight access in Boerne's historic downtown and schedule pickups to minimize disruption to your business and neighboring storefronts.
Do you offer same-day furniture removal in Boerne? +
Yes! Call us before noon and we can typically provide same-day furniture pickup anywhere in Boerne and Kendall County. Most furniture removal jobs take 30-60 minutes once we arrive, and we offer convenient 2-hour arrival windows.
What happens to the furniture you remove from Boerne homes? +
We prioritize donation and recycling. Furniture in good condition is donated to local Hill Country charities and nonprofits. Items that can't be donated are recycled whenever possible, with landfill disposal as a last resort. We'll let you know if your items qualify for donation.
