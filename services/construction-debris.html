Professional Construction Debris Removal Service

Renovating your Hill Country home or managing a construction project? The debris piles up fast. From bathroom remodels to whole-house renovations, Steady Flame Hauling and Junk Removal provides fast, reliable construction debris removal throughout Kerrville and the Texas Hill Country.

Construction waste is heavy, bulky, and dangerous. Drywall dust, sharp metal, splintered lumber, roofing shingles - this isn't the kind of material you want cluttering your property or risking injury trying to haul yourself. Plus, most of it won't fit in your regular trash service, and making repeated trips to the dump wastes your valuable time.

We work with both homeowners and contractors throughout the Hill Country. Whether you're a DIY homeowner tackling a weekend project or a professional builder managing multiple job sites, we provide the construction debris removal service you need to keep projects moving forward and sites clean.

Our team understands construction timelines. We offer flexible scheduling, including same-day service, to accommodate your project needs. We can handle one-time pickups after demo day or schedule regular debris removal for larger, ongoing projects. Serving Kerrville, Ingram, Fredericksburg, Boerne, Comfort, and all surrounding Hill Country communities.

Construction Debris Removal Pricing

Volume-based pricing that makes sense

Our construction debris removal pricing is based on volume - you pay for the space your materials take up in our truck. This is typically more cost-effective than renting a dumpster, and you don't have the hassle of permits, rental fees, or time constraints.

Typical Project Pricing:

  • Small bathroom remodel: $200-300
  • Kitchen renovation: $300-500
  • Flooring replacement (whole house): $250-400
  • Roof replacement debris: $300-600
  • Whole-house renovation: $600-1,000+
  • New construction cleanup: $400-800+

What Affects Construction Debris Removal Cost?

  • Volume of material: More debris = higher cost
  • Type of materials: Heavy materials like concrete cost more than light materials like drywall
  • Accessibility: Easy access means lower cost
  • Location: Projects within Kerrville have standard pricing; extended Hill Country locations may have a small travel fee
  • Frequency: Regular pickups for ongoing projects receive discounted rates

Construction Debris Removal FAQ

Common questions answered

How much does construction debris removal cost in Kerrville? +
Construction debris removal costs are volume-based, typically ranging from $200-800+ depending on the amount of material. A small bathroom remodel might be $200-300, while a whole-house renovation could be $600-1,000+. We provide free quotes based on your specific project.
What types of construction debris do you remove? +
We remove all types of construction materials including drywall, lumber, flooring, roofing shingles, siding, concrete, brick, tile, cabinets, fixtures, and general renovation waste. If it came from a construction or remodeling project, we can haul it away.
Do you work with contractors? +
Yes! We work with contractors throughout the Hill Country. We understand construction timelines and can schedule around your project needs. We offer competitive contractor rates and can set up regular pickups for ongoing projects.
Can you handle same-day construction debris removal? +
Yes, we offer same-day service when you call before noon, subject to availability. This is perfect for contractors who need debris cleared to keep projects moving or homeowners who want quick cleanup after demo day.
Do you recycle construction materials? +
Absolutely. We separate and recycle metals, clean lumber, concrete, and other recyclable materials whenever possible. This reduces landfill waste and is better for our Hill Country environment.
Is construction debris removal cheaper than renting a dumpster? +
Often yes, especially for smaller to medium-sized projects. You avoid dumpster rental fees, delivery charges, pickup fees, and overage charges. Plus, we do all the loading work - you don't have to fill a dumpster yourself.
What construction materials can't you remove? +
We cannot remove hazardous materials including asbestos, lead paint chips, chemical waste, or contaminated soil. These require specialized disposal through certified hazardous waste handlers. We're happy to provide referrals.
