Professional Junk Vehicle Removal Service

That old car sitting in your driveway, barn, or back pasture isn't doing you any favors. It's an eyesore, it's taking up valuable space, and in the Texas Hill Country heat, it's just rusting away. Steady Flame Hauling and Junk Removal makes it easy to get rid of unwanted vehicles with our hassle-free junk car removal service.

Junk Car Removal FAQ

Common questions about our vehicle removal service

Do I need a title to have my junk car removed? +
While having a title makes the process faster, we can often work with vehicles without titles. You'll need to prove ownership through registration, ID matching the registration, or other documentation. Contact us to discuss your specific situation.
Is junk car removal really free? +
In most cases, yes! For vehicles with salvage value (metal, parts), we can often provide free pickup. Some vehicles in very poor condition or difficult locations may have a small fee. We'll let you know upfront when you call.
Do you pay cash for junk cars? +
Depending on the vehicle's condition, make, model, and current scrap metal prices, we may be able to pay you for your junk car. Larger vehicles and those with valuable parts typically have more value. Call us for a quote on your specific vehicle.
How quickly can you pick up my junk car? +
We typically can schedule junk car pickups within 24-48 hours for the Kerrville area. Same-day pickup may be available depending on our schedule. Extended Hill Country locations may require additional scheduling time.
What condition does my car need to be in? +
We take cars in any condition - running or not, complete or missing parts, damaged, rusted, flood-damaged, or fire-damaged. The only requirement is that we can access it with our equipment.
Do I need to be present for pickup? +
If you're receiving payment, you'll need to be present to sign paperwork and receive funds. For free pickups, we can sometimes arrange for you to leave keys and signed paperwork if you can't be there. Discuss options when you schedule.
What areas do you serve for junk car removal? +
We serve all of Kerrville and the Texas Hill Country including Ingram, Hunt, Fredericksburg, Boerne, Bandera, Comfort, and surrounding rural areas. We regularly pick up vehicles from ranches and remote properties.
