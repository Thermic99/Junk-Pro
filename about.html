About Steady Flame Hauling and Junk Removal

Your local, family-owned junk removal company serving the Texas Hill Country

Our Story

Built on hard work, honesty, and Hill Country values

Steady Flame Hauling and Junk Removal was founded with a simple mission: to provide Kerrville and the surrounding Hill Country communities with honest, reliable junk removal service that you can trust. We're not a national franchise - we're your local neighbors who live and work right here in the Hill Country.

For over 2 years, we've been helping homeowners, businesses, and property managers reclaim their spaces from unwanted junk. Whether it's a single piece of furniture or a complete estate cleanout, we approach every job with the same level of professionalism and care.

What started as a small operation with one truck has grown through word-of-mouth referrals and repeat customers who trust us to do the job right. We're grateful for every opportunity to serve our community and proud to be the junk removal company that Hill Country residents recommend to their friends and family.

Our Eco-Friendly Commitment

Protecting the Hill Country we love

Living in the beautiful Texas Hill Country comes with a responsibility to protect it. That's why we're committed to eco-friendly junk removal practices that minimize waste and maximize reuse.

♻️

We Recycle

Metal, electronics, appliances, cardboard, and other recyclable materials are sorted and taken to proper recycling facilities - not dumped in landfills.

🎁

We Donate

Usable furniture, working appliances, and household goods are donated to local charities where they can help families in need and stay out of the waste stream.

🌍

We Minimize Waste

Only after we've exhausted recycling and donation options do we resort to landfill disposal - and even then, we use licensed facilities that follow environmental regulations.

Our Goal: Divert at least 60% of collected items from landfills through recycling and donation. It's good for the environment, good for our community, and the right thing to do.
