⚠ Issue Found: Quote Forms Not Working

CRITICAL: The quote request forms on the homepage and contact page are not connected to anything. When a customer fills out the form and hits "Submit," the page just reloads and their message disappears. You are likely losing leads from this.

The forms look great and the fields are all correct, but they need to be connected to a service that actually sends the submissions to your email or phone. Here are some easy options to fix this:

  • Formspree.io — Free for up to 50 submissions/month. Just change one line of code.
  • Web3Forms.com — Free, no account needed. Simple setup.
  • Netlify Forms — If your site is hosted on Netlify, it's built in.
  • Google Forms embed — Free and sends to your Google account.

This should be fixed as soon as possible so you don't lose any more potential customers.
