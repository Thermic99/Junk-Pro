How Steady Flame Hauling and Junk Removal Works

Simple, Transparent Junk Removal in 3 Easy Steps

Call Now: (830) 285-4281 Get Free Quote

Our Simple 3-Step Process

From first call to final cleanup, we make junk removal effortless

Step 1: Schedule Your Appointment

Give us a call at (830) 285-4281 or request a quote online. We'll ask about what you need removed and schedule a convenient time. Need it done today? We offer same-day service throughout the Hill Country - just call before noon!

We offer flexible scheduling with 2-hour arrival windows to fit your busy life. Weekend appointments? No problem. We're here 7 days a week, 7 AM to 7 PM.

Step 2: Get Your Free, Upfront Quote

When we arrive at your property, simply point to the items you want removed. We'll assess the volume and provide you with a clear, upfront quote right on the spot - before we touch a single item.

Our pricing is based on how much space your junk takes up in our truck. No complicated formulas, no hidden fees, no surprises. The price we quote is the price you pay, and it includes all labor, loading, hauling, and disposal.

If you approve the quote, we get to work immediately. If not, no worries - there's absolutely no obligation.

Step 3: Sit Back & Relax

Once you give us the green light, our team handles everything. We'll do all the heavy lifting, carefully load your items onto our truck, and haul everything away. We even sweep up the area when we're done, leaving your space clean and clutter-free.

You don't need to help, prepare items, or lift a finger. Many customers choose to run errands or relax while we work. That's the beauty of full-service junk removal!

After we leave, your junk is sorted responsibly - items in good condition are donated to local Hill Country charities, recyclables go to proper facilities, and only what can't be reused or recycled goes to the landfill.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our process

How quickly can you come out? +
We offer same-day service throughout the Hill Country! If you call before noon, we can typically arrive that same afternoon. We also offer flexible scheduling with convenient 2-hour arrival windows to work around your schedule.
How do you determine pricing? +
Our pricing is based on the volume of space your items take up in our truck - it's simple and transparent. When we arrive, we'll assess your items and provide you with an upfront, all-inclusive quote before we begin any work. The price we quote is the price you pay - no hidden fees, no surprises.
Do I need to be present during removal? +
You need to be present when we arrive to show us what needs to be removed and approve the quote. After that, you're welcome to stay or go - we'll handle everything and lock up when we're done if needed.
What happens to my junk after removal? +
We're committed to eco-friendly disposal. Items in good condition are donated to local charities. Recyclable materials go to recycling centers. We only send to the landfill what can't be reused or recycled, minimizing our environmental impact.
Is there anything you won't take? +
We remove most items, but we can't take hazardous materials like chemicals, paint, asbestos, or medical waste. If you're unsure about an item, just give us a call and we'll let you know.
Do I need to prepare my items before you arrive? +
No preparation needed! Just point to what you want removed, and we'll handle all the heavy lifting, loading, and cleanup. That's the beauty of full-service junk removal - you don't lift a finger.
