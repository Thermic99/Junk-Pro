Do you offer same-day junk removal in Kerrville?

Yes! As our home base, Kerrville receives priority scheduling. Call us before noon and we can typically provide same-day service. We're available 7 days a week from 7 AM to 7 PM.

What areas of Kerrville do you serve?

We serve all of Kerrville, including downtown, the Guadalupe River area, neighborhoods around Schreiner University, Peterson Medical Center area, and properties extending to the city limits and beyond into unincorporated Kerr County.

We charge based on how much space your junk takes up in our truck. We provide a free, no-obligation quote on-site before starting any work. There are no hidden fees for stairs, heavy items, or disposal. The price we quote is the price you pay.

Absolutely! We partner with local Kerrville charities and recycling centers to divert as much as possible from landfills. Usable furniture, appliances, and household items are donated to local organizations that help Hill Country families in need.

Can you remove items from inside my Kerrville home or business?

Yes! We do all the heavy lifting. Whether items are in your basement, upstairs bedroom, backyard, or garage, we'll handle everything. You just point to what you want removed, and we take care of the rest.
