Lakehills Junk Removal - Serving the Medina Lake Community

Lakehills is a Bandera County community built around one of the Texas Hill Country's most treasured resources: Medina Lake. With over 5,000 residents split between year-round families and a rotating cast of weekend visitors, this lakeside area generates a distinct mix of junk removal needs that differ from typical rural Hill Country properties. The Junk Pros provides specialized hauling and cleanout services tailored to the realities of lakefront living, vacation property maintenance, and the steady turnover that defines a recreational community.

The Unique Challenges of Lakefront Property Cleanups

Living on or near Medina Lake means dealing with situations that inland properties simply do not face. Fluctuating water levels expose debris along the shoreline that needs to be collected and hauled away. Old docks, deteriorating boat lifts, and crumbling retaining walls require heavy removal that most homeowners cannot handle alone. Boats, personal watercraft, and trailers that have outlived their usefulness sit in driveways and side yards taking up valuable space. The Junk Pros handles all of it. We have the equipment to disassemble and remove waterfront structures, haul away abandoned watercraft, and clear the accumulated clutter that builds up on lake properties over the years.

Many Lakehills properties sit on steep, sloping lots that descend from the road down to the waterline. Carrying heavy items up those grades requires physical strength and the right strategy. Our crew comes prepared with the manpower and equipment to safely remove items from any part of your property, whether it is a broken refrigerator at the top of the driveway or a collapsed deck structure down near the water.

Vacation Home and Weekend Property Specialists

A significant portion of Lakehills properties serve as vacation homes or weekend retreats for families from San Antonio, Austin, and beyond. These properties accumulate junk in their own way: guests leave behind broken outdoor furniture, storage closets fill up with items that never make it back to the primary residence, garages become dumping grounds for things that do not fit anywhere else, and outdoor recreation equipment piles up season after season. We work with vacation property owners to periodically clear out everything that has built up, restoring the property to the relaxing retreat it is supposed to be.

Because many owners are not local, we make remote coordination easy. You can describe the job by phone, have a neighbor or property manager provide access, and we handle everything from there. We send photos of the completed work so you can confirm the results without making a special trip. We also work with property managers who oversee multiple rental homes and vacation properties around Medina Lake, providing regular cleanout service on a schedule that keeps their properties guest-ready.

Serving the Full Lakehills Community

Our service area covers all of Lakehills and the surrounding Medina Lake region. We serve lakefront homes along both the north and south shores, properties near the Medina Dam, homes along FM 1283 heading toward Pipe Creek, residences near the Lakehills community center and Bandera County Park, and rural acreage properties in the surrounding hills above the lake. Whether your home has a boat ramp in the backyard or sits on ten quiet acres above the lake, we provide the same reliable, professional service.

What Lakehills Homeowners Need Removed Most

After years of serving the Medina Lake area, we know what tends to pile up on Lakehills properties. Old outdoor and patio furniture is the single most common item we remove - the sun, humidity, and occasional flooding take a heavy toll on deck chairs, umbrellas, tables, and grills. Hot tubs and above-ground pools that have reached the end of their life are another frequent request. We also regularly remove old appliances from lake houses being remodeled, haul away construction debris from renovation projects, clear out garages and storage buildings packed with years of accumulated belongings, and dispose of damaged fencing and landscaping materials after storms.

Storm and Flood Debris Specialists

Medina Lake and the surrounding Hill Country are no strangers to severe weather. Heavy rains can cause rapid water level changes, flooding low-lying properties and depositing debris across lakefront lots. High winds bring down trees and scatter loose items across the landscape. When storms hit Lakehills, we respond quickly to help property owners clear debris, remove damaged structures, and restore their properties. We prioritize storm response calls because we understand the urgency of getting your home back to normal after a weather event.

Boat and Watercraft Removal

Medina Lake is a fishing and boating destination, and that means there are always boats, jet skis, kayaks, and trailers that have seen their last trip on the water. Whether it is a bass boat with a cracked hull sitting on a trailer in your driveway or a personal watercraft rusting behind the garage, we handle the removal. We also take old boat docks, floating platforms, and marine equipment that is no longer serviceable. Recycling metals from these items is always part of our process.

Fair, Transparent Pricing for Lake Properties

Lakehills is approximately 35 miles south of our Kerrville headquarters, and it falls within our standard Bandera County service territory. There are no distance surcharges, no extra fees for steep lakefront lots, and no markups for difficult access. You pay based on the volume of junk we haul away - that is the only variable. We provide free on-site estimates before starting work, and the number we quote is the number you pay. Loading, hauling, disposal, and cleanup are all included.

Protecting Medina Lake Through Responsible Disposal

Medina Lake is the lifeblood of the Lakehills community, and protecting its water quality matters to everyone who lives here. We take environmental responsibility seriously on every job. Usable items are donated to local charities. Metals, appliances, and recyclable materials go to certified facilities. We never leave materials where they could wash into the lake during the next rain, and we take extra care on waterfront properties to prevent any debris from entering the water. The Junk Pros treats Medina Lake with the same respect that the community does.

Lake communities thrive on trust and reliability. When you call The Junk Pros, you get a team that shows up when promised, works efficiently, handles your property with care, and leaves your lot cleaner than when we arrived. We are licensed, insured, and experienced with the specific challenges that lakefront and vacation properties present. Whether you need a single item picked up or an entire property cleared out, Lakehills can count on us.
