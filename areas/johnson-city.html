Professional Junk Removal Services in Johnson City, Texas

Steady Flame Hauling and Junk Removal is proud to serve Johnson City and the surrounding northern Blanco County area with fast, affordable junk removal. Sitting about 50 miles east of our Kerrville base at the intersection of Highway 290 and Highway 281, Johnson City is best known as the boyhood home of President Lyndon B. Johnson. But beyond its presidential history, this town of roughly 2,000 residents has become one of the Hill Country's most dynamic small communities, blending old-time ranch culture with a booming wine country scene and a steady influx of weekenders from Austin and San Antonio. Whatever brings junk into your life here, Steady Flame Hauling and Junk Removal hauls it out.

From Presidential History to Modern Hill Country Living

Johnson City's heritage runs deep. The LBJ National Historical Park draws visitors year-round to the Lyndon B. Johnson boyhood home and the Johnson Settlement, giving the town a sense of place that few small Texas communities can match. The homes and properties surrounding the historic district reflect that heritage, with many structures dating back generations. Families who have lived here for decades often need help with estate cleanouts when loved ones pass on or when it is finally time to clear out an attic, barn, or storage building that has been accumulating belongings for years.

We treat every estate cleanout in Johnson City with the care it deserves. Our team understands that the items we are removing often carry personal significance, and we work respectfully and efficiently to help families through the process. We can sort, haul, and dispose of everything from furniture and clothing to old tools, appliances, and general household accumulation.

Serving Johnson City's Wine Country Boom

The stretch of Highway 290 between Johnson City and Fredericksburg has become one of the premier wine trails in Texas. Wineries like Texas Hills Vineyard, along with dozens of tasting rooms, event venues, and boutique lodging properties, have transformed the local economy. This growth means constant construction, renovation, and property turnover, all of which generate junk removal needs.

We work with winery owners, tasting room operators, and event venue managers who need to clear old fixtures, demolition debris from renovations, broken barrels and equipment, and outdated furnishings. We also serve the growing number of vacation rental and Airbnb properties in the Johnson City area. When property owners need to swap out mattresses, haul away worn furniture, or clear an entire rental unit for a refresh, Steady Flame Hauling and Junk Removal gets it done quickly so there is minimal downtime between bookings.

Ranch Properties and Rural Acreages

Outside the town center, Johnson City is surrounded by ranches, hobby farms, and large acreage properties that stretch toward the Pedernales River and out into the rolling Hill Country landscape. These properties accumulate junk differently than suburban homes. Old ranch equipment, collapsed outbuildings, rusted stock tanks, miles of broken fencing, scrap lumber, and decades of miscellaneous debris can pile up across a property over the years.

Steady Flame Hauling and Junk Removal has the heavy-duty trucks and experienced crew to handle large rural cleanups that smaller haulers simply cannot manage. Whether you are preparing a ranch property for sale, cleaning up after storm damage, or just finally tackling that barn full of junk you have been meaning to deal with for years, we can provide an honest quote and get the job done.

Pedernales Falls and Outdoor Recreation Properties

Pedernales Falls State Park, located just east of Johnson City, is one of the most popular outdoor destinations in the Hill Country. The neighborhoods and properties along the Pedernales River corridor between town and the park include vacation cabins, river houses, and residential properties that regularly need junk removal services. From hauling away old dock materials and broken outdoor furniture to clearing brush and storm debris from riverside lots, we handle the unique needs of properties in this scenic area.

The Science Mill and Community Growth

Johnson City has also gained attention through attractions like the Science Mill, a hands-on science museum housed in a restored 1880s cotton gin. The presence of the Science Mill, along with the LBJ sites and wine trail, has attracted new businesses, restaurants, and shops to the town center. As the commercial district grows and evolves, local business owners turn to Steady Flame Hauling and Junk Removal to remove old retail fixtures, restaurant equipment, office furniture, and general business clutter when they open, renovate, or close up shop.

Frequently Asked Questions - Johnson City Junk Removal

How far is Johnson City from your Kerrville base?

Johnson City is approximately 50 miles east of Kerrville via Highway 290. We travel this route regularly and serve the entire Johnson City and northern Blanco County area. There is no extra travel surcharge for most jobs in Johnson City.

Can you help with cleanouts for properties near the LBJ National Historical Park?

Yes! Many of the homes and properties near the LBJ National Historical Park and along the Pedernales River are older and often need estate cleanouts, renovation debris removal, or general decluttering. We handle these jobs with care and respect for the historic character of the area.

Do you serve the wineries and commercial properties around Johnson City?

Absolutely. Johnson City's growing wine country industry means tasting rooms, event venues, and commercial properties regularly need junk removal. We handle old fixtures, broken equipment, pallets, packaging waste, and full commercial cleanouts for businesses along Highway 290 and throughout the area.

Yes, hot tub removal is one of our specialties. Many Hill Country properties around Johnson City have aging hot tubs, above-ground pools, and outdoor equipment that need professional removal. We disassemble and haul everything away in a single visit.

In most cases, yes. We can often provide same-day or next-day service to Johnson City. Call us early in the day for the best availability. We are open 7 days a week from 7 AM to 7 PM.

What if I have a large ranch cleanout near Johnson City with multiple outbuildings?
