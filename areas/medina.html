Serving Medina's Apple Country

Medina's claim to fame is its apple orchards, which draw visitors every fall harvest season. We work with orchard owners and agricultural properties throughout the area, handling removal of old equipment and machinery, clearing storage buildings and barns, hauling away agricultural debris and materials, and removing outdated harvest equipment. Beyond orchards, Medina is home to country properties, riverside homes along the beautiful Medina River, small ranches and homesteads, and seasonal vacation properties. We serve all property types with the same professional, reliable service that Hill Country residents have come to trust.

Comprehensive Rural Junk Removal Services

Living in rural Medina often means accumulated items in multiple locations - the main house, storage buildings, barns, and workshops. We handle it all. Our services include furniture and appliance removal from homes, agricultural equipment and machinery hauling, barn and outbuilding cleanouts, estate cleanouts with compassion, construction and renovation debris, vehicle and trailer removal, yard waste and brush clearing, and riverside property cleanup after flooding or storms.

We're equipped for the challenges of rural service - navigating unpaved roads, accessing properties across acreage, working around agricultural operations, and respecting the quiet, peaceful nature of country living. Our team treats your property with care and leaves it cleaner than we found it.

Eco-Friendly Disposal Practices

Medina residents appreciate the natural beauty of their rural community and the importance of good stewardship. We share those values.
