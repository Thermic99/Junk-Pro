Professional Junk Removal Services in Blanco, Texas

The Junk Pros proudly serves Blanco and all of Blanco County with dependable, affordable junk removal. Nestled along the Blanco River about 45 miles east of Kerrville, Blanco is the kind of small Hill Country town where everybody knows each other and the historic courthouse square still anchors daily life. With a population of around 2,000, Blanco may be small, but its residents and growing number of visitors deserve top-notch service when it comes time to clear out unwanted junk. That is exactly what we deliver.

A Town Rich in History and Character

Blanco has served as the Blanco County seat since 1890, and the town's identity is built around its beautiful limestone courthouse square. The Old Blanco County Courthouse, now operating as a visitor center and community hub, stands as a testament to the town's deep roots in Hill Country history. Many of the homes and buildings surrounding the square date back over a century, and property owners in these older structures frequently need help clearing out decades of accumulated belongings, outdated furniture, and renovation debris from restoration projects.

Whether you live in one of the classic stone cottages near the square, a riverside property along the Blanco River, or a newer home on the outskirts of town along Ranch Road 165 or Highway 281, The Junk Pros can handle your removal needs. We understand the unique challenges of working with historic Hill Country properties, including narrow lots, gravel driveways, and older structures that require careful navigation.

Serving Blanco's Growing Tourism Economy

Over the past decade, Blanco has transformed from a quiet ranching community into a popular Hill Country destination. The lavender farms along Highway 281 draw thousands of visitors each spring and summer, and the surrounding wine country has fueled a boom in vacation rentals, bed-and-breakfasts, and short-term rental properties. This growth means constant turnover of furnishings, mattresses, and appliances as property owners refresh their rental spaces to keep up with guest expectations.

We work regularly with vacation rental owners and property managers in the Blanco area who need fast, reliable junk removal between guest turnovers. Whether you are swapping out a worn-out sofa set, replacing outdated kitchen appliances, or clearing an entire unit for a remodel, The Junk Pros can get it done on your schedule. We also serve the local shops and businesses around the courthouse square that occasionally need to clear old inventory, fixtures, or equipment.

Ranch and Rural Property Cleanups

Blanco County remains deeply rooted in agriculture and ranching. Many properties outside the town limits sit on large acreages with barns, outbuildings, and workshops that accumulate junk over the years. Old fencing materials, broken ranch equipment, scrap metal, lumber piles, and general debris can pile up fast on a working property. Our crew has the trucks and manpower to tackle large-scale rural cleanups that smaller haulers cannot handle.

We also assist with brush and yard waste removal for property owners maintaining their land along the Blanco River corridor and the rolling hills south of town toward Wimberley. If you have storm debris, cleared cedar, or landscaping waste that needs hauling, give us a call.

Blanco State Park and Riverside Properties

Blanco State Park, located right in the heart of town along the Blanco River, is one of the area's most beloved attractions. The neighborhoods surrounding the park and lining the river include some of the most desirable properties in town. Homeowners in these areas take pride in their properties, and when it is time to declutter a garage, clean out an attic, or haul away old patio furniture and hot tubs, they turn to The Junk Pros for professional, respectful service that gets the job done without disrupting their neighborhood.
