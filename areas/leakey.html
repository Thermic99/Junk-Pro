Serving Leakey and the Frio Canyon

Leakey sits at the heart of the Frio River canyon, surrounded by some of the most stunning scenery in Texas. The area attracts visitors year-round, which means many properties serve as vacation rentals, second homes, or recreational properties. We understand the unique needs of Leakey property owners - from seasonal cleanouts to preparing rentals for the busy summer tubing season.

As the county seat of Real County, Leakey serves a wide rural area with ranches, river properties, and small acreages. We're equipped to handle junk removal in this diverse community, from downtown Leakey to remote canyon properties along the Frio.

Why Leakey Property Owners Trust The Junk Pros

Owning property in the Frio Canyon comes with special considerations. Many homes and cabins here have been in families for generations, accumulating furniture, equipment, and memories over the decades. When it's time to clear things out - whether for a renovation, an estate settlement, or simply to declutter - you need a junk removal service that will treat your property with respect.

We also work with vacation rental owners who need quick turnarounds between guests or seasonal cleanouts to freshen up their properties. Whatever your situation, we provide professional, reliable service with upfront pricing and no surprises.

Frequently Asked Questions - Leakey Junk Removal

How far in advance should I schedule Leakey pickups?

We recommend scheduling Leakey pickups 2-3 days in advance when possible. This allows us to coordinate trips efficiently. For urgent needs, call us - we'll do our best to accommodate you.

Do you service properties along the Frio River?

Yes! We serve properties throughout the Frio canyon area. Just let us know about access conditions when you call - some river properties have unique access considerations we need to plan for.

Can you help with vacation rental cleanouts?

Absolutely! We work with vacation rental owners for seasonal refreshes, furniture replacement, and between-guest cleanouts. We can work with your schedule to minimize disruption to bookings.

Is there an extra charge for Leakey service?

Due to the distance, there may be a travel fee for Leakey pickups. We'll always provide an upfront quote so you know the total cost. For larger jobs, the travel fee becomes a smaller percentage of the overall cost.
